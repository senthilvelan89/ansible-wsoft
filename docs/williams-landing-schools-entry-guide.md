# Schools near 12 Shepherd Street, Williams Landing

A practical guide to **P–9, 7–12 and P–12** government, Catholic and independent schools within about **5–7 km** of **12 Shepherd Street, Williams Landing VIC 3027**, plus how each school actually admits students, and where families can get help for competitive exams.

Researched **September 2026**. Distances below are **straight-line** from the house (OpenStreetMap). Driving time is longer, especially on Palmers Road at drop-off. **Always confirm the current zone** for this exact address and enrolment year at [findmyschool.vic.gov.au](https://www.findmyschool.vic.gov.au/) before you apply. Zones change.

This is **not** enrolment advice from a school or the Department. Policies, fees, test dates and capacity change every year. Ring the school before you rely on any row.

## Entry rules in plain English

### Government schools (almost all rows in the first table)

NAPLAN is **not** an entry test for a normal government place.

Under the Victorian Department of Education [Placement Policy](https://www2.education.vic.gov.au/pal/enrolment/guidance/placement-policy):

- Every eligible child has a **right** to their designated neighbourhood (zoned) school.
- Schools **must not** ask for school reports, NAPLAN, teacher judgement or interviews **before** they make a placement offer.
- Out-of-zone applications are taken only if there is room. Priority is:
  1. Sibling at the same permanent address who will still be at the school
  2. Everyone else, by **closeness** of the home to the school
- Compassionate grounds can apply in exceptional cases.

Some growing-area schools (Saltwater P–9 is one) have an extra **enrolment restriction**. For 2027 Saltwater only takes in-zone students, siblings, or compassionate cases. Others may be tight even without a published restriction.

**Year 7 for 2027** (government): if the child is in a Victorian government primary in 2026, the primary gives out the Year 6–7 pack in Term 2. Typical dates this cycle: apply by **Friday 8 May 2026**; offers **Wednesday 24 June 2026**. You can list up to three government secondaries. Check [Moving from primary to secondary school](https://www.vic.gov.au/moving-primary-secondary-school-information-parents-and-carers) for the live dates.

A **High Achievers / Everest / SELP** class is a **separate** process. You usually need the school place first, then sit EduTest, an internal English/Maths exam, or ACER.

### Catholic colleges

Priority is typically **siblings**, then **baptised Catholic** students from feeder parish primaries, then other applicants if there is space. Emmanuel and Thomas Carr ask for recent **school reports and NAPLAN as documents**, not as a pass/fail public exam. Thomas Carr’s **Magis** stream *does* use NAPLAN, PAT and a college test **after** you are in the enrolment pool.

Williams Landing sits near the **Thomas Carr** (Tarneit) and **Emmanuel Notre Dame** (Point Cook) catchments. Emmanuel’s published parishes are the Altona / Laverton / Williamstown group. If the house is treated as Thomas Carr or MacKillop territory, Emmanuel may want **written parish-priest permission**. Ask both registrars.

### Independent schools

Places are **application + capacity + often a placement test**. NAPLAN and reports are usually uploaded with the form. They are used for placement and scholarships, not as the statewide Year 7 process.

## Better Education scores (2025)

[Better Education](https://bettereducation.com.au/schoolranking.aspx) publishes two different numbers. Do not mix them up.

| Number | What it is | Scale | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **State Overall Score** | Years 7–10 academic rating (NAPLAN-based). This is the “score” on their suburb maps. | **60–100**. 100 is the top of the Victorian list. 60 is the floor. **80 is mid-pack, not 80%.** | Comparing P–9 / 7–10 campuses |
| **VCE median study score** | Year 12 results. State median is about **30**. | Usually mid-20s to high 30s. **% of scores 40+** is the high-achiever share. | Comparing schools that go to Year 12 |

**Read the scores with these caveats**

- **Suzanne Cory’s 100 is selective intake**, not a neighbourhood-school comparison. Students already sat an exam to get in.
- A high score often tracks **family background (SES / ICSEA)** as much as teaching. Westbourne (98) and Alamanda (94) serve more advantaged catchments than Laverton (73).
- P–9 schools have **no VCE**. Senior colleges (Point Cook Senior, Tarneit Senior) have **no Years 7–10 score**.
- New schools (**Bemin**, **Yurran**) have no published score yet.
- This is **not** an entry test for a government place. A zoned child still gets in regardless of the school’s rating.
- Figures below are **2025** from Better Education suburb rating pages (Point Cook 3030, Hoppers Crossing / Tarneit / Truganina 3029, Altona 3018). Recheck the live page; they update after each NAPLAN / VCE release.

## Competitive pathways (what the exam actually is)

NAPLAN is a **national literacy/numeracy snapshot** in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. It is **not** the Victorian selective-school exam and **not** the way a government school offers a zoned place. Independent and Catholic schools often **collect** the latest NAPLAN PDF with the application. Only a few local **streams** (Thomas Carr Magis; some scholarship shortlists) treat those scores as selection data.

| Pathway | Who sits it | Test | When (typical) | NAPLAN used? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Suzanne Cory / Melbourne High / Mac.Rob / Nossal** Year 9 | Year 8 students (citizenship / visa rules apply; fee-paying international students are ineligible) | **ACER SEHS**: Reading reasoning, Maths reasoning, Verbal GA, Quantitative GA, Writing. About 3 hours. Knowledge capped at Year 8. | Applications ~March–April; exam **June** (2027 entry exam was **20 June 2026**). Offers in rounds. | **No.** Exam rank is the main filter. |

### Suzanne Cory — extra rules that catch families out

- Statewide process: [Selective entry high schools](https://www.vic.gov.au/selective-entry-high-schools) and [ACER portal](https://vic.registration.selectiveentry.acer.org).
- About **1,000 Year 9 places across four schools**. Suzanne Cory takes ~**200**.
- Offer mix: **85% exam rank**, up to **10% equity** (Health Care / Pensioner card or Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander), up to **5% principal discretion** (invitation only after the exam).
- **Source-school cap**: no more than **4%** of a Year 8 cohort from one school (or **10%** if that school is P–9 only) for standard + principal-discretion offers. Equity offers sit outside the cap.
- You can sit the Year 9 exam **once**.
- Practice materials: buy official ACER SEHS practice from ACER. Third-party “NAPLAN coaching” is the wrong exam.

**Official / cheap first steps (do these before paying for a year of coaching)**

1. **ACER Selective Entry** practice tests — only paper that matches Suzanne Cory Year 9.
2. **EduTest** sample tests — Westbourne, Werribee SELP, Alamanda Everest, ICOM SAP, mid-entry Cory.
3. **ICAS** past papers — Al-Taqwa SEEP only.
4. School-published HAP sample tasks (Point Cook P–9 / Alamanda) if they release any.
5. Keep **Year 5–8 classroom maths and reading** strong. SEHS does not go past Year 8 content; it rewards reasoning speed and writing under time.

If the goal is **only** a zoned government P–9 or P–12 place, you do **not** need exam tutoring. Spend the money on the school’s own transition, or on tutoring only if the child is aiming at Cory / Westbourne / Magis / Everest / SELP.

## Suggested paths from 12 Shepherd Street

**Need a guaranteed government seat (Years 7–9)**  
Check Find my School. Historically the suburb has been split across **Laverton P–12**, **Dohertys Creek P–9** and **Carranballac P–9**. If one of those is the designated school, that is the no-exam path.

**Want one campus through to Year 12 (government)**  
Closest is **Laverton P–12** (~1.6 km). Next government 7–12 options: **Hoppers Crossing SC**, **Bemin**, then **The Grange**. After a Point Cook P–9, families usually move to **Point Cook Senior Secondary**.

**Want one campus through to Year 12 (fee-paying)**  
Closest: **Westbourne Grammar** and **Al-Taqwa** (both Sayers Road, ~3 km, bus 150). Then **Emmanuel Notre Dame** (Catholic, 2.6 km), **Thomas Carr** (Catholic, 5 km), **Heathdale**, **ICOM**, **Good News**.

**Want a high-ability government stream without leaving the west**  
Year 7–8: Alamanda Everest, Point Cook HAP, or Werribee SELP (further, but accredited).  
Year 9: **Suzanne Cory** via the ACER exam (~6 km). Start serious prep in Year 6–7 if that is the target; applications open when the child is in Year 8.

**Catholic with an academic stream**  
Thomas Carr **Magis** is the local one that explicitly uses NAPLAN + a college test. Emmanuel is faith/sibling priority, not an exam school.
