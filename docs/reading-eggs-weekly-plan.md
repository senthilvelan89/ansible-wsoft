# Grade 1 Reading Eggs week

Monday–Saturday: **15 minutes in the app**, then **5–10 minutes with you**.  
**Sunday: no study.**

Login → **Start Learning** → open **only** the program for that day.

If there is no Mathseeds, do Tuesday and Thursday with a ten-frame and counters.

| Day | App (15 min) | Focus | With you (5–10 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | **Fast Phonics** → **Peaks** | Sounds | She says 4 sounds, writes the letters, blends 3 words (*s-a-t*). |
| **Tuesday** | **Mathseeds** → **Lessons** | Number | Same number with counters: “How do you see it? Make 10.” |
| **Wednesday** | **Reading Eggs** → **Lessons** | Words | She reads the lesson book out loud, finger under each word. No guessing from the picture. |
| **Thursday** | **Mathseeds** → next **Lesson** | Adding / taking away | 4 spoken problems: “You had 10, I ate 3. How many? How do you know?” |
| **Friday** | **Library** (one book + quiz) | Meaning | “What happened? Why did they…?” |
| **Saturday** | **Spelling** (or Driving Tests) | Write the sounds | You say 4 words from the week; she writes. Tick each sound. |
| **Sunday** | **No study.** App closed. | Day off | Nothing. |

Monday–Saturday bedtime: you can still read a story to her. That is not homework.  
Sunday: no app, no phonics, no maths, no catch-up.
